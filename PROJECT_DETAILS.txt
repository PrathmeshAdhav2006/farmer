1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROBLEM STATEMENT
================================================================================
1.1 The Agricultural Crisis: Middlemen Exploitation & Information Asymmetry
In traditional Indian agricultural supply chains (particularly in Maharashtra), 
farmers face severe systemic challenges:
  a) Intermediary Exploitation: Middlemen (arhatiyas, brokers, dalals) take 
     upwards of 40% to 60% of consumer purchase prices, leaving farmers with 
     slender margins that often fail to cover seed, fertilizer, and labor costs.
  b) Price Opacity: Farmers lack real-time access to accurate Agricultural 
     Produce Market Committee (APMC) mandi trading rates, forcing them to sell 
     distress crops below true market worth.
  c) Lack of Traceability: Consumers pay premium prices for produce labeled 
     "organic" or "farm-fresh" without any verifiable proof of origin, harvest 
     timelines, or chemical-free practices.
  d) High Wholesale Barriers: Small households cannot access bulk wholesale 
     discounts, and farmers cannot afford small-order shipping costs.
  e) Delayed & Insecure Payments: Farmers frequently face payment defaults 
     and delayed cash collections from commission agents.

1.2 The Solution: Farmer Marketplace
Farmer Marketplace is an open, direct-to-consumer (D2C) and business-to-business 
(B2B) agricultural platform designed specifically for Maharashtra's agricultural 
ecosystem. It connects farmers directly with retail consumers, cooperative 
housing societies, restaurants, and bulk buyers.

Key Highlights of the Platform:
  - Disintermediation: 100% direct trade between farmers and buyers.
  - Transparent Mandi Pricing: Integrated 90-day APMC market pricing benchmarks 
    across 7 key Maharashtra agricultural districts.
  - Community Group-Buy Deals: Social commerce enabling neighborhood buyers 
    to pool orders together and unlock wholesale prices.
  - Digital Product Passport: End-to-end crop traceability detailing farm 
    taluka/village origin, harvest date, quality grade, and eco-metrics.
  - Secure Financial Transactions: Razorpay payment gateway integration, Cash 
    on Delivery (COD), and automated Escrow holding for large orders (> Rs. 5000).
  - Real-Time Communication: Direct WebSocket chat between farmer and buyer.
  - Robust Image Fallbacks: Zero broken images via an intelligent multi-tiered 
    fallback engine (Unsplash produce CDN -> local uploads -> SVG fallback).
  - Clean, Deterministic Engineering: No fragile external black-box models or 
    third-party OAuth bottlenecks; reliable, self-contained architecture.

4. FUNCTIONAL MODULES & DEEP DIVE FEATURES
================================================================================

4.1. Authentication & Profile Management
- Local Credentials Auth: User accounts are protected using industry-standard 
  bcrypt salt hashing (10 rounds). Passwords are never stored in plain text.
- JWT Session Management: Successful authentication returns a cryptographically 
  signed JSON Web Token (HS256) valid for 7 days. Tokens are stored in browser 
  localStorage and forwarded in the `Authorization: Bearer <token>` header.
- Granular Role Registration:
  * Farmers register with specific agricultural metadata: district, taluka, 
    village, and farm size in acres.
  * Buyers register with mobile number, default delivery addresses, and city.
  * Admins possess platform oversight privileges.
- Password Recovery: Token-based forgot/reset password email workflow powered 
  by Nodemailer with single-use hashed recovery tokens.

4.2. Farmer Product & Inventory Management
- Rich Crop Cataloging: Farmers list produce specifying crop name, category 
  (vegetables, fruits, grains, pulses, spices, dairy, other), quantity available, 
  price per kg, quality grade (Grade A, Grade B, Grade C), harvest date, and 
  projected shelf expiry date.
- Organic Verification: Checkbox indicating certified organic produce with 
  accompanying certification details.
- Multi-Image File Uploads: Supports up to 5 images per product. Handled via 
  Multer. Gracefully falls back to local storage in `server/uploads` if Cloudinary 
  credentials are not configured.
- Real-Time Inventory Control: When orders or group-buys are placed, stock 
  levels deduct atomically to prevent overselling.

4.3. Digital Product Passport & Supply Chain Traceability
- Concept: An automated transparency badge attached to every crop listing.
- Origin Verification: Displays the exact district, taluka, and village where 
  the crop was harvested, linking directly to the farmer's profile.
- Freshness Index: Calculates elapsed days since harvest date to inform buyers 
  of real product freshness.
- Eco-Sustainability Metrics: Auto-computes carbon footprint reduction from 
  direct localized delivery compared to multi-tier middleman transport.
- Quality Grading Audit: Indicates quality inspection benchmarks (Grade A 
  export quality, Grade B standard market, Grade C processing grade).

4.4. APMC Mandi Market Prices (Analytics & Trends)
- Mandi Price Transparency: Direct integration with Agricultural Produce Market 
  Committee records across Maharashtra.
- Covered Districts: Nashik, Pune, Nagpur, Aurangabad, Kolhapur, Solapur, Sangli.
- Key Commodities: Tomato, Onion, Potato, Grapes, Pomegranate, Sugarcane, 
  Wheat, Rice, Soybean, Cotton.
- Visual Charts: Interactive Recharts visualizations rendering 90-day price 
  trajectories, modal prices, daily arrival quantities, and minimum/maximum 
  trading ranges.
- Farmer Benefit: Farmers can cross-reference current market price ceilings 
  before listing their own produce to avoid under-pricing.

4.5. Community Group-Buy Deals (Social Commerce)
- How It Works: A farmer can launch a time-limited "Group-Buy Deal" for 
  harvest surpluses (e.g. 500 kg of Nashik Tomatoes at Rs. 20/kg instead of Rs. 40/kg).
- Threshold Quota: The deal defines a target quantity and expiration date.
- Community Participation: Neighborhood buyers and housing societies join the 
  deal, pledging quantities (e.g. 20 kg, 50 kg).
- Deal Unlocking: When the collective quota is achieved before expiry, the deal 
  locks as "Achieved", converting into confirmed discounted orders. If the quota 
  is not met, participating buyers are refunded without penalty.

4.6. Shopping Cart & Multi-Item Checkout
- Cart Management: Powered by React `CartContext` with real-time backend 
  synchronization. Buyers can update quantities and remove items.
- Multi-Farmer Checkout: Supports adding produce from different farmers into 
  a single consolidated cart. The backend splits sub-orders per farmer.
- Saved Address Selector: Allows buyers to store multiple addresses (Home, Work, 
  Apartment) and choose the active delivery destination.
- Logistics Partner Simulation: Displays delivery partners (e.g., Krishi Express, 
  Kisan Logistics, Maharashtra Agro Transport) with calculated delivery fees.

4.7. Order Lifecycle, Logistics & Escrow Payments
- Order States:
  [ PENDING ] -> [ CONFIRMED ] -> [ PROCESSING ] -> [ PACKED ] -> [ SHIPPED ] -> [ DELIVERED ]
       |
       +---> [ CANCELLED ]
- Payment Modes:
  1. Cash on Delivery (COD) for convenience.
  2. Online Payment via Razorpay Gateway (UPI, Credit/Debit cards, Netbanking).
- Escrow Protection: For wholesale orders exceeding Rs. 5,000, funds are held 
  in platform escrow upon payment and released to the farmer only once the 
  buyer confirms satisfactory delivery, eliminating transaction fraud.

4.8. Real-Time Chat & Messaging System
- Direct Channel: Socket.io powered WebSocket connection directly between 
  buyers and farmers.
- No Middlemen: Buyers can negotiate custom bulk quantities, inquire about 
  harvest freshness, and arrange transport directly.
- Chat Persistence: Chat rooms and message histories are archived in MongoDB 
  collections (`chatrooms` and `messages`) so conversations persist across sessions.

4.9. Real-Time Push & Email Notification Engine
- In-App Alerts: Instant notifications broadcasted via Socket.io when an order 
  is placed, updated, cancelled, or when a chat message arrives.
- Transactional Emails: HTML email templates dispatched via Nodemailer for:
  * Welcome verification
  * Order placement confirmation (with breakdown table)
  * Farmer order arrival notification
  * Password reset tokens

4.10. Interactive Farm Map (Geo-Location)
- Visual Farm Discovery: Built using React Leaflet and OpenStreetMap.
- Map Markers: Pins farmers across Maharashtra districts showing their active 
  farm locations, farm sizes, cultivated crops, and verification status.

4.11. Admin Administration & Moderation Console
- Platform Analytics: Global metrics displaying Total Gross Merchandise Value (GMV), 
  active user counts, crop listing volumes, and completed order totals.
- User Verification: Review farmer profiles, verify land documents, and toggle 
  active/suspended account statuses.
- Product Moderation: Approve new listings, highlight featured crops, or take 
  down violating inventory.

7. WEBSOCKET (SOCKET.IO) EVENT SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================
The Socket.io server initializes on top of the Express HTTP server on port 5000.

7.1. Connection & Room Scoping
- Connection: Client connects sending auth handshake credentials.
- Event: `join (userId)`
  * Client emits its unique `userId`. The server creates/joins a dedicated socket 
    room named `userId`. This ensures private notifications are delivered exclusively 
    to the target user without leaking data to other connections.

7.2. Chat Messaging Events
- Event: `send-message`
  * Payload: `{ recipientId, roomId, message, sender: { _id, fullName } }`
  * Server emits `receive-message` to room `recipientId`.
- Event: `typing`
  * Payload: `{ roomId, senderId }`
  * Broadcasts typing status indicator to the other participant.

7.3. Push Notification Broadcasts
- Event: `notification`
  * Dispatched by `notificationService.send()` on backend business actions.
  * Triggered on: Order created, order status updated, group deal locked, 
    verification approved, new review posted.

9. RESILIENCE, IMAGE FALLBACKS & MEDIA HANDLING
================================================================================
A core engineering achievement in this system is its Multi-Tier Fallback Engine 
that completely eliminates broken image links (`404` errors):

1. Primary: If a Cloudinary CDN image URL exists, it is loaded.
2. Local Multer Uploads: If running offline or without Cloudinary keys, the 
   backend automatically stores uploaded photos in `farmer/server/uploads/` 
   and serves them through Express static middleware at `http://localhost:5000/uploads/...`.
3. Crop & Category CDN Fallbacks: When a product has no image or an empty array, 
   the client utility `getProductImageUrl(url, category)` analyzes the crop name 
   or category and loads an authentic, curated produce photo:
   - Tomato    -> High-res ripe tomato photo
   - Onion     -> High-res sun-cured onion photo
   - Mango     -> High-res Alphonso mango photo
   - Grapes    -> High-res Thompson vineyard photo
   - Spinach   -> High-res organic leafy green photo
   - Potato    -> High-res fresh potato photo
   - Chilli    -> High-res spicy green chilli photo
   - Coconut   -> High-res tender coconut photo
   - Cashew    -> High-res raw cashew nut photo
   - Category Fallbacks -> Vegetables, Fruits, Grains, Pulses, Spices, Dairy.
4. Terminal Fallback: All `<img />` tags across the frontend are equipped with 
   `onError={(e) => { e.currentTarget.src = getProductImageUrl('', category) }}` 
   falling back to a local vector SVG asset (`/placeholder.svg`) ensuring 
   an image is always visible even under zero network connectivity.
